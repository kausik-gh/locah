# FOUNDER REFINEMENT — PAYMENTS

Payments should remain simple.

The Business decides what it wants to collect:

- full payment
- advance
- deposit
- part payment
- balance later
- COD / pay on delivery
- pay on pickup
- cash at counter
- UPI
- external card terminal
- recurring payment where supported

The payment model should adapt to the business workflow.

Examples:

Restaurant / shawarma:
usually full payment or COD.

Hotel:
deposit now, balance later.

Salon / clinic:
optional booking deposit.

Gym:
full membership fee or recurring payment.

Custom cake / project:
advance now, balance later.

Retail/POS:
cash, UPI, card, split tender.

B2B:
credit / invoice / later settlement belongs to the B2B + ledger flow, not normal
consumer checkout.

================================================================
1. CUSTOMER MONEY GOES TO THE BUSINESS
================================================================

CUSTOMER → BUSINESS merchant payment
must remain separate from
BUSINESS → LOCAH platform billing.

LOCAH records what the payment is for.

It must not treat merchant revenue as LOCAH revenue.

Current selected provider direction is Cashfree through the provider-neutral
payment architecture.

================================================================
2. PAYMENT STATES
================================================================

Keep payment state separate from order/booking/membership state.

At minimum:

UNPAID
PENDING
PAID
FAILED
PARTIALLY_PAID
REFUNDED
PARTIALLY_REFUNDED
CANCELLED / EXPIRED where provider semantics support it.

Do not treat browser redirect as final payment truth.

Provider verification / webhook is authoritative.

================================================================
3. FAILED PAYMENT
================================================================

FAILED should give the customer a clear:

Retry payment

action.

Retry should create/use a safe new payment attempt without duplicating the
business transaction.

The Order / Booking / Membership remains the same transaction.

Only the payment attempt changes.

Do not create a second order merely because payment failed.

================================================================
4. PENDING PAYMENT
================================================================

PENDING requires extra care.

Do NOT immediately create another payment attempt while the previous one may
still succeed.

First:

check provider status.

If still genuinely pending:
show:

Payment is still being confirmed.

Actions may include:
- Check again
- Cancel attempt where provider permits
- Try another method only after the previous attempt is safely expired/cancelled

If the provider later reports SUCCESS:
mark the transaction paid exactly once.

If the provider reports FAILED / EXPIRED / CANCELLED:
allow retry.

No double charge.

No duplicate payment record.

Use idempotency throughout.

================================================================
5. ADVANCE / DEPOSIT / FULL PAYMENT
================================================================

The Business owner configures what applies.

Examples:

FULL PAYMENT
₹1,000 now.

ADVANCE
₹300 now
₹700 balance later.

PERCENTAGE ADVANCE
30% now.

FIXED DEPOSIT
₹500 deposit.

PAY LATER
₹0 now where Business allows it.

LOCAH calculates and displays:

Total
Paid
Balance due

clearly.

Do not force all businesses into one payment pattern.

================================================================
6. WEBSITE
================================================================

Website checkout should feel normal:

Review transaction
→ choose payment method
→ pay
→ provider verification
→ confirmation.

If payment fails:
Retry.

If pending:
show pending honestly and verify status.

If deposit/advance is configured:
show exactly what is being paid now and what remains.

================================================================
7. WHATSAPP
================================================================

WhatsApp can send a secure payment link tied to the real:

Order
Booking
Membership
Invoice
Outstanding balance.

The customer pays through the payment page/provider.

WhatsApp itself is not payment truth.

After webhook verification:
the same underlying transaction becomes paid/part-paid.

================================================================
8. POS
================================================================

POS supports the actual counter methods enabled by the Business:

Cash
UPI
external card terminal
split tender
other explicitly supported modes.

Dynamic UPI QR:
exact amount.

Cash:
cash received / change.

External card terminal:
record terminal payment according to the verified current architecture.

Split example:

₹1,000 total

₹400 cash
₹600 UPI.

The bill/invoice sees the combined settlement.

================================================================
9. REFUNDS
================================================================

Refunds must always link to an actual prior payment.

Support:
full refund
partial refund

only where the real provider/payment method permits it.

Refund state must not silently change the original payment history.

Keep:

original payment
refund transaction
resulting net paid amount.

Money movement/refund permissions remain server-side and audited.

AI cannot independently issue unrestricted refunds.

================================================================
10. BALANCE COLLECTION
================================================================

If only advance/deposit was paid:

LOCAH should always know:

Total amount
Amount paid
Balance due.

The remaining amount can later be collected through:

payment link
counter payment
cash
UPI
other enabled method.

Examples:

Hotel:
deposit now → balance at check-in/checkout.

Project/custom work:
advance → milestone → final balance.

Appointment:
deposit → balance after service.

================================================================
11. RECURRING PAYMENTS
================================================================

Where supported and legally/provider-allowed:

Memberships
recurring delivery
service contracts
fee plans
member dues

may use recurring/autopay.

If autopay fails:
do not silently cancel the underlying membership unless the configured
membership lifecycle says so.

Instead use the Membership/Collections rules:

failed charge
→ reminder/retry
→ grace period where configured
→ status change according to owner policy.

================================================================
12. PAYMENT RECEIPT / INVOICE
================================================================

Payment is not the same as Invoice.

Payment:
money movement / settlement.

Invoice:
commercial/tax document.

A payment can settle:
Order
Booking
Membership
Invoice
Donation
Deposit
Outstanding ledger amount.

The appropriate receipt/invoice should be generated through the existing
Invoicing system.

Do not create separate Website-payment receipts outside the shared document
system.

================================================================
13. CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE
================================================================

The customer should always understand:

Amount due
Amount paying now
Payment method
Payment status
Amount already paid
Balance remaining.

Never expose confusing provider terminology.

Use simple states such as:

Payment received
Payment failed — try again
Payment still being confirmed
Balance ₹___ remaining.

================================================================
14. OWNER EXPERIENCE
================================================================

The owner should see:

Paid today
Pending payments
Failed payments needing attention
Outstanding balances
Refunds
Payment method
Linked order/booking/invoice/member
provider reference where useful.

No fake revenue.

Only verified payment state contributes to paid revenue metrics.

================================================================
15. SECURITY / RELIABILITY
================================================================

Every payment attempt needs:

business_id
customer / transaction link
amount
currency
payment purpose
provider
provider reference
idempotency key
attempt status
verified timestamps
audit trail.

Webhook replay must never duplicate a payment.

A delayed success from an older attempt must be reconciled safely.

Never rely only on frontend state.

================================================================
16. FINAL PRINCIPLE
================================================================

Payments should be boring and reliable.

The complexity belongs in the Business workflow:

Order
Booking
Membership
Hotel stay
POS
Invoice
Donation
B2B Ledger

Payments simply answers:

How much is due?
How much is being collected now?
How is it being paid?
Did the provider actually confirm it?
How much remains?

Keep it simple.
Keep it auditable.
Never double-charge.
