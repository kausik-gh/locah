# FOUNDER REFINEMENT — MEMBERSHIPS & SUBSCRIPTIONS
## ONE RECURRING-RELATIONSHIP ENGINE, DIFFERENT BUSINESS EXPERIENCES

LOCAH should NOT build completely separate engines for:

Gym Membership
Milk Subscription
Tiffin Subscription
Coaching Fees
PT Session Pack
AMC
Club Dues

They share a recurring relationship foundation.

But they MUST NOT look like the same product to the owner or customer.

Use:

ONE RECURRING RELATIONSHIP ENGINE
+
PLAN KIND
+
SCHEDULE
+
ENTITLEMENT
+
PAYMENT MODEL
+
BUSINESS TYPE / TRAITS
+
ROLE

→ SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE.

================================================================
1. MEMBERSHIP VS SUBSCRIPTION
================================================================

Use different CUSTOMER/OWNER LANGUAGE even when the backend is shared.

MEMBERSHIP

Usually means:

customer gets access, eligibility or participation for a period.

Examples:

gym membership
yoga membership
club membership
co-working membership
academy/coaching enrolment relationship.

SUBSCRIPTION

Usually means:

something repeatedly happens for the customer.

Examples:

daily milk
breakfast subscription
lunch subscription
dinner subscription
tiffin
flowers
newspaper-like product
produce box
pet-food repeat delivery
other recurring store deliveries.

SESSION PACK

Customer buys a limited quantity of usage.

Examples:

10 PT sessions
8 yoga classes
5 salon sessions
therapy/service package.

FEE PLAN

Customer remains enrolled and pays according to an instalment schedule.

Examples:

coaching academy
tuition centre
education fees.

SERVICE CONTRACT / AMC

Customer is covered for a duration and receives defined service visits.

Examples:

AC AMC
RO service contract
pest-control contract
IT maintenance contract.

MEMBER DUES

Examples:

club
association
trust
community organisation.

Use familiar words in each experience.

A milk customer should see:

"My Subscription"

not:

"My Membership".

A gym user should see:

"My Membership".

A coaching parent may see:

"Fees / Enrolment"

rather than:
"Subscription".

2. COMMON ENGINE
================================================================

Every recurring relationship should be able to track, where applicable:

customer
plan
kind
start date
end date
status
price
billing frequency
current paid period
next due date
payment status
renewal
pause/freeze
grace period
history
created channel
location

Never overwrite history when renewed.

Example:

Jan membership
Feb membership
Mar membership

must remain three periods/history entries,
not one row whose dates keep changing forever.

================================================================
3. COMMON STATUS LOGIC
================================================================

Use deterministic lifecycle states.

Conceptually:

PENDING_PAYMENT
ACTIVE
PAUSED
GRACE
EXPIRED
CANCELLED

plus kind-specific derived conditions where needed.

"Expiring soon" should be a derived condition based on the date,
not a separate permanent state.

The system must always be able to answer:

Is this customer active now?

When does it expire?

How many days remain?

When is the next payment?

What happens if they do not pay?

What have they already paid?

Has the subscription been paused?

================================================================
4. CONTINUOUS / FREQUENT TRACKING
================================================================

This is important.

LOCAH must never depend on the owner remembering to check expiry dates manually.

Lifecycle should update automatically.

Use BOTH:

EVENT-DRIVEN CHECKING

Whenever something happens:

payment received
payment failed
check-in
delivery generated
freeze
resume
renewal
cancel
session used

recalculate the relationship immediately.

AND

SCHEDULED LIFECYCLE CHECK

At least daily according to the Business timezone:

find:
expiring soon
due today
overdue
grace
expired
renewal due
deliveries due
fee instalments due
AMC visits due.

Do NOT require the owner to press:

"Refresh memberships".

Do NOT run wasteful minute-by-minute polling for things that change by day.

Use deterministic scheduled jobs + events.

5. RENEWAL TRACKING
================================================================

The owner should immediately see:

Active
Expiring soon
Due today
Payment pending
In grace
Expired
Paused.

The canonical source already defines a Members board, renewal calendar and
member timeline for exactly this purpose.

Renewal reminders should be automated.

Typical configurable ladder may include:

2 days before
expiry day
grace-period warning
expired.

Exact reminder schedule is owner-configurable.

Once renewed:
future obsolete reminders must stop automatically.

No duplicate reminders.

================================================================
7. FREEZE / PAUSE
================================================================

Useful for gym and similar memberships.

If owner policy allows:

member freezes for 10 days.

Then the membership end date should extend by exactly the allowed frozen period
according to the plan rules.

Track:

freeze start
freeze end
reason
who approved it.

Do not silently alter expiry without history.

================================================================
8. SESSION PACKS
================================================================

Example:

10 PT sessions.

Track BOTH:

expiry date
AND
remaining sessions.

Active only while rules allow both.

Example:

Purchased: 10
Used: 6
Remaining: 4.

Each eligible booking/completed session consumes according to configured rules.

Cancellation/no-show behavior should follow the Business policy.

Do not let Bookings maintain a separate session count.

Membership/session-pack is the entitlement truth.

Bookings asks it:

Can this customer book?
How many sessions remain?

product/menu item
quantity

Examples:

Monday–Friday.

Lunch only

LOCAH must generate the required daily operational orders/delivery work

================================================================
10. DAILY CUTOFF
================================================================

Recurring deliveries should have a configurable cutoff.

Example:

9 PM today
→ finalize tomorrow's subscriptions.

At cutoff LOCAH determines:

active subscriptions
minus pauses
minus skips
plus temporary quantity changes

→ tomorrow's required deliveries/orders.

This then feeds:

Orders
Kitchen where relevant
Inventory/Recipes
Procurement
Dispatch.

Example:

250 active lunch subscribers
15 paused
8 skipped tomorrow

Tomorrow's lunch requirement:

227.

That number should automatically become operational demand.

================================================================
11. CUSTOMER SKIP / PAUSE
================================================================

Recurring delivery customers need very easy control.

Website/My Activity/WhatsApp:

Skip tomorrow
Pause from 4–8 Oct
Resume
Change quantity
Change permitted delivery days
Cancel subscription

subject to Business rules/cutoff.

Example:

"Skip milk tomorrow"

should update tomorrow's demand BEFORE cutoff.

If after cutoff:
show the Business's actual policy instead of silently changing an already
prepared/delivered order.

================================================================
12. TEMPORARY CHANGES
================================================================

Very important for milk/food.

Customer normally receives:

1 litre milk.

Tomorrow they want:

2 litres.

That should be:

one-occurrence override.

Do not permanently change their recurring subscription unless they explicitly
choose:

"Change future deliveries".

Likewise:

usual lunch = 1 meal.

Friday only = 3 meals.

Recurring schedule remains intact.

================================================================
13. PREPAID VS POSTPAID SUBSCRIPTIONS
================================================================

Support both when the Business allows them.

PREPAID

Customer pays:
week/month/period.

Deliveries occur while subscription remains eligible.

POSTPAID

Example traditional milk delivery:

deliver every day
→ accumulate actual supplied quantities
→ skips do not get charged
→ month-end statement/bill
→ customer pays balance.

The canonical source explicitly expects the milkman-style month-end billing
model.

Ledger owns outstanding balance.

Membership/Subscription owns the delivery relationship.

Orders own generated daily fulfilment.

================================================================
14. FOOD SUBSCRIPTIONS
================================================================

For tiffin/cloud kitchen/meal businesses:

subscription might specify:

Breakfast
Lunch
Dinner

or combinations.

Each meal can have appropriate:

menu/choice
delivery slot
address
quantity
dietary preference only where the Business actually supports it.

Daily operational view should answer:

How many breakfasts today?
How many lunches?
How many dinners?
Who skipped?
Who is paused?
Which addresses/routes?
Which payments are overdue?

Kitchen receives actual required production counts.

Procurement sees resulting ingredient requirements through Recipes/BOM.

Dispatch gets actual delivery jobs.

Subscription remains the schedule source.

================================================================
15. STORE PRODUCT SUBSCRIPTIONS
================================================================

Recurring delivery should not be limited to food.

It can apply to any suitable Business-configured product.

Examples:

milk
eggs
water cans
flowers
pet food
produce box
household consumables
other repeat goods.

Do not hard-code:
subscription = milk/tiffin.

If the owner has a suitable recurring product,
they can configure it.

LOCAH recommendations should suggest it for appropriate businesses.

16. EDUCATION / COACHING
================================================================

Education should feel like:

ENROLMENT + FEE PLAN

not gym membership.

Track:

student
guardian/payer where appropriate
course/batch relationship
fee schedule
instalments
due dates
paid
outstanding
grace/late rules according to owner policy
start/end/term.

Example:

Course fee ₹30,000.

₹10,000 admission
₹10,000 due 1 Nov
₹10,000 due 1 Dec.

LOCAH should show:

Paid ₹20,000
Outstanding ₹10,000
Next due 1 Dec.

Do not automatically remove the student from enrolment merely because a payment
is overdue unless the owner's written rule says so.

Attendance is a separate module.

Academics is a separate module.

Membership/Fee plan owns the commercial recurring relationship.

18. AMC / SERVICE CONTRACT

19. CLUB / ASSOCIATION / COMMUNITY DUES

================================================================
20. AUTOPAY
================================================================

If supported and enabled:

renew automatically through the approved payment provider.

If autopay succeeds:
create/extend next period exactly once.

If autopay fails:

do NOT immediately corrupt/expire the membership.

Follow configured lifecycle:

payment failed
→ retry/payment link
→ grace if configured
→ expiry when grace ends.

Webhook replay must never extend the membership twice.

================================================================
21. EARLY RENEWAL
================================================================

If membership ends 31 Dec and customer renews on 20 Dec:

do NOT reset membership to start on 20 Dec and lose 11 days.

Queue/extend the next period after the current one.

Example:

Current:
1 Dec → 31 Dec.

Renewed early for one month:

Next:
1 Jan → 31 Jan.

History stays intact.

29. FINAL PRINCIPLE
================================================================

Memberships and subscriptions are NOT identical in customer meaning.

But they share one recurring relationship foundation.

A gym owner thinks:

members
check-ins
renewals
expiry.

A milkman thinks:

daily customers
tomorrow's quantity
skips
monthly bill.

A coaching academy thinks:

students
fee instalments
term ending.

An AMC business thinks:

contracts
service visits
renewal.

A club thinks:

members
dues
good standing.

LOCAH should understand all of them without creating five unrelated systems.
