# FOUNDER REFINEMENT — INVENTORY
## ONE STOCK ENGINE FROM A SMALL SHOP TO A SUPERMARKET TO INDUSTRIAL/B2B

Inventory must be one of LOCAH's most reusable operational capabilities.

It should work from:

- a tiny provision shop
- kirana
- supermarket
- meat/fish shop
- bakery
- restaurant
- shawarma/QSR shop
- pharmacy
- cosmetics shop
- fashion/footwear
- electronics/mobile shop
- hardware store
- wholesale/distribution
- field-service van
- garage/spare-parts business
- warehouse
- industrial supplier
- manufacturing/material-based business

without creating a different inventory backend for every business.

The rule is:

ONE INVENTORY TRUTH
+
BUSINESS TRAITS
+
STOCK MODEL
+
OFFERING TYPE
+
LOCATION
+
ROLE
+
OPERATING WORKFLOW

→ SPECIALIZED INVENTORY EXPERIENCE.

Inventory should be UNIVERSAL-CAPABLE, not UNIVERSALLY MANDATORY.

A consultant/accountant/lawyer may not need it.

But any business that owns, stores, consumes, transforms, reserves, transfers,
sells or carries physical stock should be able to use the same Inventory engine.

================================================================
1. POS / BILLING AND INVENTORY ARE DIFFERENT BUT CONNECTED
================================================================

POS/Billing records the commercial sale.

Inventory records stock truth.

Example:

Provision shop:
scan biscuit
→ POS sale
→ stock decreases by 1.

Electronics:
scan phone + IMEI
→ POS sale
→ exact serial becomes sold
→ stock decreases
→ warranty lookup is possible.

Meat shop:
sell 2.5 kg chicken
→ invoice/POS
→ stock decreases by 2.5 kg.

Restaurant:
sell one shawarma
→ POS/order
→ recipe/BOM consumes:
  chicken
  pita
  sauce
  vegetables
→ ingredient inventory decreases.

Never maintain:
pos_stock
website_stock
whatsapp_stock

There is ONE stock truth.

All channels update it through the same inventory service/event path.

================================================================
2. DIFFERENT INVENTORY MODES
================================================================

Inventory behavior should be composed from real stock characteristics.

SIMPLE QUANTITY STOCK

Example:
biscuit packet
water bottle
soap
book
hardware item.

Track:
SKU
quantity
location
reorder level.

WEIGHT-BASED STOCK

Example:
rice
meat
fish
vegetables
sweets.

Track:
kg/g/ml/litre as appropriate
purchased unit
selling unit
conversion.

YIELD / TRANSFORMATION STOCK

Example:
whole chicken
fish
bakery dough
prepared food.

Example:

10 kg whole chicken
→ 7 kg curry cut
→ 2 kg boneless
→ 1 kg trim/waste.

Owner records the actual yield.

Inventory must reflect:
input
usable outputs
wastage
remaining stock.

VARIANT STOCK

Example:
shirt
shoe
helmet
cosmetic shade.

One product may contain:

size × colour
or
capacity × colour
or other source-supported variants.

Each sellable SKU has its own quantity.

BATCH / EXPIRY STOCK

Example:
medicine
milk
packaged food
cosmetics.

Track:
batch
received quantity
remaining quantity
manufacture/expiry where available.

Sale/issue should use FEFO:
FIRST EXPIRY FIRST OUT.

Show:
expiring soon
expired
batch quantities.

SERIALISED STOCK

Example:
phones
electronics
appliances
certain machinery/parts.

Track individual:
serial number
IMEI where relevant
sale
customer
warranty relationship.

Sale requires/selects the serial where configured.

MULTI-LOCATION STOCK

Example:
supermarket chain
warehouse
franchise
multi-branch retailer.

Stock is never merely:
"100 units".

It is:
Location A: 30
Location B: 50
Warehouse: 20.

Transfers must have real states:

requested
approved where required
in transit
received.

Do not instantly subtract from one branch and magically add to another without
representing transit.

VAN / MOBILE STOCK

Example:
field technician
route salesman
service van.

A vehicle/assigned stock location may carry parts.

Technician can consume parts against a job.

Route salesman can sell from van stock.

INGREDIENT / MATERIAL STOCK

Example:
restaurant
bakery
shawarma shop
manufacturer.

Finished-product demand can consume underlying ingredients/materials through
Recipe/BOM.

Do not force owners to manually reduce ingredients after every sale.

================================================================
3. CORE STOCK TRUTH
================================================================

For any stock item, LOCAH should be able to derive the applicable concepts:

on hand
reserved
available to promise
incoming/confirmed inbound where Procurement exists
reorder level
location
unit
cost/value where tracked

plus, only when relevant:

batch
expiry
serial
variant
yield
wastage
recipe/BOM relationship.

Do not show irrelevant fields.

A small provision shop should not see:
yield
BOM
serial controls

unless configured.

A meat shop should not be forced through an electronics-style serial screen.

A restaurant should not treat every ingredient like a finished retail SKU.

================================================================
4. RECEIVE STOCK
================================================================

Receiving stock should be easy for the smallest shop but capable of supporting
larger businesses.

Simple shop:

Receive:
Coca-Cola 500ml
Qty 24
Cost ₹...
Done.

Batch business:

Receive:
Item
Batch
Expiry
Qty
Cost.

Serialised business:

Receive:
Product
Qty
Serial/IMEI list
Cost.

Weight-based business:

Receive:
Chicken
25 kg
Cost/kg.

Supplier/Procurement integration later:

Purchase Order
→ Goods Receipt
→ actual quantity
→ Inventory increase.

Never increase stock merely because a PO was sent.

Stock increases when goods are actually received.

================================================================
5. SALES AND RESERVATIONS
================================================================

When an online/WhatsApp order is confirmed and source rules require reservation:

reserve the necessary quantity.

Reserved stock must not be offered to another customer as freely available.

If order is cancelled:
release reservation.

If fulfilled:
reservation becomes real stock deduction.

POS immediate sale:
deduct according to sale path.

Returns:
add back stock only according to the valid return condition and original stock
identity.

For serialised items:
the same sold serial should return.

For batch-aware stock:
return should preserve appropriate batch provenance where available.

================================================================
6. STOCK COUNTS
================================================================

A physical stock count must be a normal workflow.

Example:

LOCAH says:
Coke = 50.

Staff counts:
47.

Do NOT silently overwrite.

Create:

Expected: 50
Counted: 47
Variance: -3

with reason/approval according to configured policy.

Small business:
simple full count.

Supermarket:
count by category/aisle/section.

Warehouse/multi-location:
count one location independently.

The resulting adjustment becomes an audited stock movement.

================================================================
7. WASTAGE / WRITE-OFF
================================================================

Businesses must be able to record stock lost for operational reasons.

Examples:

expired
damaged
spoiled
trim loss
breakage
other configured reason.

Food/fresh businesses should see wastage prominently.

A normal electronics shop should not have wastage as its main screen.

Wastage decreases stock and preserves an audit trail.

For yield-driven businesses, wastage contributes to understanding actual yield.

================================================================
8. REORDER / SHORTAGE
================================================================

Every applicable item/location may have owner-configured:

minimum
target / maximum where used
safety stock where used.

Example:

Current usable stock = 8
Minimum = 10
Target = 30

LOCAH should surface:

Low stock
Suggested replenish quantity = 22

This becomes an INPUT to Procurement.

Inventory should not itself become the full purchasing system.

Inventory determines:

WHAT is low / required.

Procurement decides:

WHO to buy from
WHAT PO to create
approval
delivery
goods receipt
supplier bill.

This must connect later to the finalized B2B/ChitBridge procurement logic.

================================================================
9. INVENTORY + PROCUREMENT / B2B
================================================================

Inventory is one input to the procurement calculation.

Example:

Confirmed demand = 100
Usable inventory = 70
Confirmed inbound = 10
Desired safety stock = 5

Net requirement:

100 + 5 - 70 - 10
= 25.

Procurement then proposes buying 25, adjusted for:
supplier pack
MOQ
supplier rules.

Inventory must expose accurate availability to Procurement.

Do not duplicate inventory calculations inside Procurement.

================================================================
10. RESTAURANT / SHAWARMA / FOOD SERVICE
================================================================

A shawarma shop is a perfect Inventory case.

Customer buys:
1 chicken shawarma.

They are billed through POS / Orders.

Recipe may contain:
120 g chicken
1 pita
30 g sauce
20 g cabbage.

That sale should consume those ingredients from stock.

Inventory experience should emphasize things such as:

Ingredients
Today's available stock
Low ingredients
Wastage
Recipe consumption
Expected requirement
Receiving
Stock count

rather than a generic retail-product table.

For restaurants:
same principle at larger scale.

Orders/POS
→ Kitchen
→ Recipes/BOM
→ Inventory
→ Procurement.

The canonical source explicitly expects restaurant sales to connect to
recipe-based stock and supplier purchasing.

================================================================
11. PROVISION STORE / KIRANA
================================================================

Primary experience:

Products
Barcode/SKU
On hand
Reserved
Available
Expiry/batches where relevant
Low stock
Receive
Count stock
Reorder.

Counter billing and online/WhatsApp ordering must hit the SAME quantity.

If the owner has Tally or another verified billing system as stock master,
follow configured source-of-truth rules instead of creating competing quantities.

================================================================
12. SUPERMARKET
================================================================

Same Inventory engine as kirana, but deeper operational presentation:

many SKUs
categories/departments
multiple counters
multiple storage locations where configured
batches/expiry
receiving
cycle counts
replenishment
transfers
fast-moving/low-stock views derived from real data.

Do not create a "supermarket inventory" backend.

Scale the same engine.

Use pagination/search/scanning rather than loading thousands of rows at once.

================================================================
13. MEAT / FISH / FRESH
================================================================

Primary experience should emphasize:

available weight
whole stock
prepared cuts
yield
wastage
today's requirement
receiving
reorder/procurement.

Example:

Whole chicken received: 100 kg.

Processing results:
Curry cut: 70 kg
Boneless: 20 kg
Trim/waste: 10 kg.

Those outputs become the sellable stock where the business actually operates
that way.

Website, WhatsApp and counter must all see the same available quantities.

================================================================
14. PHARMACY / EXPIRY-HEAVY STOCK
================================================================

At Inventory level only:

batch
expiry
FEFO
near-expiry alerts
quantity per batch.

Do NOT let this become clinical/HIMS functionality.

Inventory tracks products.

It does not create:
patient records
diagnoses
prescriptions
clinical decisions.

================================================================
15. FASHION / FOOTWEAR
================================================================

Primary inventory view should be a useful variant matrix.

Example:

            S   M   L   XL
Black       2   5   4   1
Blue        0   3   7   2

Staff should immediately see which variant is low.

POS and Website select the same SKU/variant.

Returns/exchanges must update the exact variant.

================================================================
16. ELECTRONICS / APPLIANCES
================================================================

Primary experience:

Product/model stock
Serial/IMEI
Sold/available state
Warranty lookup
Receiving
Reorder.

At sale:

select/scan exact serial.

Customer history and invoice should be able to identify the exact sold unit.

Return should restore the correct serial if accepted.

================================================================
17. FIELD SERVICE / GARAGE
================================================================

Inventory should support:

central store
technician/van stock
parts consumed on job
unused parts returned
replenishment.

Example:

Technician uses:
1 capacitor
2 metres cable

against Job #123.

Those parts leave the assigned stock location and become job consumption.

Do not ask the technician to separately edit stock afterward.

================================================================
18. INDUSTRIAL / MANUFACTURING-LITE
================================================================

Within LOCAH's source-defined boundary:

Raw materials
Components
Finished goods
Lots/batches where applicable
BOM relationship
Material consumption
Receiving
Stock counts
Transfers
Shortage
Procurement requirement.

Customer orders may create material requirements through BOM.

Do NOT turn this into full MRP or shop-floor scheduling.

If a business already uses a full ERP/MRP:
LOCAH should integrate according to source-of-truth rules instead of competing
with it.

================================================================
19. MULTI-LOCATION
================================================================

Every stock movement needs a location context where relevant.

Support:

shop
warehouse
branch
van/mobile location.

A transfer is a real movement.

Source:
Warehouse A.

Destination:
Shop B.

During transit:
do not count the same stock as freely available in both locations.

Receiving completes the destination movement.

================================================================
20. TALLY / EXISTING SYSTEMS
================================================================

Inventory must respect ONE source of truth per data family.

Possible setup:

LOCAH owns stock
→ pushes to Tally.

Or:

Tally owns stock quantities
→ LOCAH synchronizes them
→ LOCAH manages safe local reservations between syncs as defined by source.

Never allow LOCAH and another system to independently overwrite stock forever.

Owner configuration decides ownership.

Sync conflicts must be visible/audited.

================================================================
22. ROLES
================================================================

Owner / Manager:
whole-business stock, value, exceptions, approvals.

Store keeper:
receiving, counts, transfers, stock adjustments.

Cashier:
sale-time stock information; serial capture where required.

Kitchen:
ingredient availability relevant to production/prep.

Technician:
only assigned van/location/job parts.

Picker:
items needed for orders.

Do not expose unnecessary cost/value information to roles without permission.

RLS/server permissions still enforce security.

================================================================
23. INVENTORY AUTOMATIONS
================================================================

Use the shared automation kernel.

Useful deterministic triggers include:

low stock
expiring stock
count variance awaiting approval
transfer awaiting receipt
reorder suggestion.

Do not use AI for the stock arithmetic.

AI Inventory Manager may later explain/prioritize suggestions,
but deterministic stock truth remains authoritative.

================================================================
24. INSIGHTS
================================================================

Only compute from real inventory data.

Useful examples where supported by actual records:

stock on hand
stock value
low-stock count
near-expiry stock
wastage
count variance
stock movement
fast/slow movement
yield variance

Do not invent percentages for empty businesses.

================================================================
25. INVENTORY MUST SCALE WITHOUT BECOMING COMPLEX FOR SMALL SHOPS
================================================================

Tiny shop experience:

Add item
Receive quantity
Sell
See remaining stock
Low-stock alert
Count stock.

The owner should not need to understand:

FEFO
BOM
valuation methodology
serial tracking
yield

unless their business needs those capabilities.

Supermarket/warehouse/industrial businesses can progressively use the deeper
features.

This is Progressive Complexity.

Same engine.
Different depth.

================================================================
26. WHAT INVENTORY MUST NOT BECOME
================================================================

Do not build:

separate inventory systems for each vertical
full ERP
full MRP
shop-floor scheduler
clinical/pharmacy patient system
duplicate stock copies for Website/POS/WhatsApp
generic huge CRUD screens
fake stock/value metrics.

Inventory owns STOCK TRUTH.

Orders own customer orders.
POS owns cashier sale workflow.
Recipes/BOM owns composition.
Procurement owns purchasing.
Dispatch owns delivery.
Invoicing owns billing documents.
Connectors synchronize external systems.

They cooperate through stable services/events.

================================================================
27. PLAYWRIGHT ACCEPTANCE
================================================================

Before Inventory is source-complete, test real browser flows for at least:

1. Small provision shop
   receive 24 units
   → POS sells 2
   → stock shows 22.

2. Online order
   → quantity reserved
   → available quantity decreases
   → cancel
   → reservation releases.

3. Kirana / supermarket
   barcode item
   → batch/expiry where applicable
   → stock count
   → variance approval.

4. Meat shop
   receive weighted stock
   → cut/yield transformation
   → wastage
   → sell a cut by weight.

5. Restaurant / shawarma
   sell finished menu item
   → recipe ingredients decrease.

6. Pharmacy-type stock
   two batches
   → earliest-expiry valid batch picked first
   → expiry alert.

7. Fashion
   variant matrix
   → exact size/colour sale
   → correct SKU decreases.

8. Electronics
   receive serialised item
   → POS captures serial/IMEI
   → warranty lookup finds sale
   → return restores correct serial.

9. Multi-location
   transfer A → B
   → stock becomes in-transit
   → B receives
   → quantities correct.

10. Field-service van
    central stock → van
    → technician uses part on job
    → van quantity decreases.

11. Reorder
    stock falls below threshold
    → suggested requirement is produced
    → feeds Procurement, not a duplicate PO system.

12. RLS / permissions
    Business A can never read or mutate Business B stock.

13. Desktop + 390 px mobile
    specialized primary Inventory views actually render correctly.

14. Full regression:
    POS
    Orders
    Invoicing
    returns
    Website availability
    Procurement integrations where present
    must still agree on the same stock truth.

Screenshots must be inspected.

================================================================
28. FINAL INVENTORY PRINCIPLE
================================================================

Inventory should feel:

Simple enough for a one-counter provision store.

Powerful enough for a supermarket.

Natural for a meat shop.

Ingredient-aware for a shawarma shop or restaurant.

Expiry-aware for pharmacy/food.

Variant-aware for fashion.

Serial-aware for electronics.

Mobile-stock-aware for technicians.

Material-aware for industrial/manufacturing businesses.

But underneath all of them:

ONE INVENTORY DOMAIN.
ONE STOCK LEDGER.
ONE QUANTITY TRUTH.
ONE LOCATION MODEL.
ONE PERMISSION MODEL.

Specialization changes the WORK EXPERIENCE,
not the underlying truth.
