# FOUNDER REFINEMENT — ORDERS & CUSTOMER TRANSACTIONS

Authority: founder instruction (authority 1), recorded 2026-09-29. It ranks
with the other founder refinements in this folder (Inventory, Bookings,
Memberships & Subscriptions, Payments, B2B / ChitBridge), above the Business
OS Guide PDF and the Business Capability Universe MD. Ledger section A2
(`tools/ledger/rows_refinements.py`, rows FR-OR-*) lists each requirement below
with its status against the code.

================================================================
ORDERS / CUSTOMER TRANSACTIONS
================================================================

LOCAH must accept customer business through all appropriate channels:

- Business Website
- Marketplace
- WhatsApp
- physical store / POS
- human-entered phone order
- AI-assisted phone order where explicitly enabled
- QR / table ordering
- repeat/reorder
- governed external billing/connectors

BUT not every customer action is an Order.

Correct routing remains:

buy product/food → Orders
appointment/service slot → Bookings
hotel stay → Bookings
gym plan/access → Memberships
outstanding bill → Payments / Invoice / Ledger
RFQ/custom B2B sale → Quotes / B2B sales workflow
site visit → Lead + Booking
donation → Donations
repair/service work → Jobs/Bookings according to configured workflow

All surfaces use the SAME module services.

Never create private copies such as:

website_orders
whatsapp_orders
phone_orders
pos_orders.

Orders may store source/channel, but operational truth remains one.

================================================================
WEBSITE CHECKOUT
================================================================

Website product/food purchase must provide a real customer checkout:

catalog/menu
→ item/options/variant/weight/modifiers
→ cart
→ fulfilment choice
→ address/details where required
→ configured delivery/other charges
→ payment choice
→ final server-side price/stock/tax validation
→ customer confirmation
→ payment where applicable
→ order confirmation
→ My Activity/tracking.

Cart is not yet an Order.

Server is authoritative for:

price
tax
discount validity
stock
availability
delivery charge
commercial total.

================================================================
OWNER ORDER SETTINGS
================================================================

Business owner configures only relevant order behavior:

channels
pickup
delivery
dine-in
shipping
COD
online payment
minimum order
delivery area
delivery charge
free-delivery threshold
preorder rules
cutoff
preparation lead time
cancellation

tax/GST

Do not show every setting to every business.

================================================================
PAYMENT AND INVOICE
================================================================

Website payment goes through the Business's merchant payment path.

CUSTOMER → BUSINESS merchant money
remains separate from
BUSINESS → LOCAH platform billing.

Payment status remains separate from order status.

Website Order and Invoice are distinct records.

Order = operational/commercial commitment.
Invoice = billing/tax document.

Use shared Invoicing.

================================================================
POS
================================================================

POS is the counter workflow:

scan/search
→ cart
→ quantity/variant/serial/weight
→ payment/tender
→ bill
→ inventory movement
→ receipt.

Counter sales must participate in unified Business/customer history.

================================================================
WHATSAPP
================================================================

WhatsApp is a CHANNEL, not a second order system.

Structured or AI WhatsApp ordering:

catalog/menu
→ lines/options
→ cart
→ fulfilment
→ address
→ payment/COD
→ explicit customer confirmation
→ SAME Orders service.

AI never invents:

price
stock
discount
delivery charge.

================================================================
PHONE ORDERS
================================================================

Human phone order:

staff creates the same Order using current catalogue, stock, pricing, tax,
delivery and payment rules.

AI phone ordering is founder-approved where safe and explicitly enabled.

For appropriate simple orders:

AI Receptionist may:

read real catalogue
construct cart
ask required options
re-check availability
read back full order + total
obtain explicit confirmation
send digital payment link
create audited actor_type=ai_employee order.

Never collect payment credentials by voice.

Complicated/custom/negotiated/high-risk/low-confidence orders hand off to a
human or WhatsApp confirmation flow.

================================================================

prepare now

================================================================
PARTIAL AVAILABILITY, EDITS, CANCELLATION, RETURNS, REORDER
================================================================

Partial availability must never silently alter the customer's purchase.

Offer appropriate:

quantity reduction
remove unavailable item
alternative
preorder/wait
talk to business

then recalculate and obtain reconfirmation.

Order edits must revalidate:

price
stock
tax
delivery
payment difference

and preserve audit history.

Cancellation:

release stock reservation
react to preparation/dispatch
handle refund through Payments
notify customer.

Returns/exchanges:

link to original transaction
preserve exact SKU/variant/serial/batch where applicable
update Inventory correctly
create refund/credit note through proper modules.

Reorder:

uses previous order as convenience only.

Re-check CURRENT:

catalogue
price
stock
availability
delivery rule

then show the new total and ask for confirmation.

================================================================
ORDERS WORKSPACE
================================================================

Orders Workspace should combine operational work rather than separate tabs for:

Website Orders
WhatsApp Orders
Phone Orders
Marketplace Orders.

Channel is a FILTER / provenance field.

Primary workflow should instead adapt to business:

Kirana:
picking / ready / delivery / pickup.

Meat:
new / cut-prep / weight exceptions / ready.

Shawarma/QSR:
new / preparing / ready / pickup / delivery.

Restaurant:
dine-in / takeaway / delivery / KDS.

Bakery:
today / tomorrow / custom preorders.

Retail/e-commerce:
processing / shipping / returns.

Do NOT use one generic CRUD table for every business.

Bookings, Memberships, Payments, Invoicing, Inventory, Kitchen, Fulfilment and
Dispatch remain separate domains connected to Orders.

================================================================
B2B
================================================================

B2B is also a separate operational EXPERIENCE.

Do not clutter consumer Orders with:

RFQs
MOQs
credit terms
supplier chits
B2B price lists

unless the configured business actually uses B2B.

Committed B2B sale may reuse shared underlying order primitives where sensible,
but B2B UI/workflow follows the founder B2B/ChitBridge refinement.

================================================================
WHICH ORDER FEATURES A BUSINESS SEES
================================================================

LOCAH recommendation rules determine which order features are shown.

Use:

category
+ owner-confirmed traits
+ operating model
+ enabled modules
+ readiness

not hard-coded business-name spaghetti.

Example:

shawarma:
Orders + POS + Kitchen + delivery/pickup + Payments etc.

kirana:
POS + Inventory + Orders + WhatsApp + Khata etc.

hotel:
Bookings primary; Orders not forced.

consultant:
Leads/Bookings/Quotes/Projects; retail Orders not forced.

NOTE ON SOURCES (recorded with this refinement)
================================================================

SOURCE_CONFLICT (resolved by authority order): the Capability Universe MD
§11.2 lists the AI Receptionist's tools and ends "Nothing else" — no
catalogue, cart or order-creation tool — and §11.3 says a restaurant's
receptionist "for phone orders sends a WhatsApp order link instead of taking a
long order by voice". This refinement authorises AI phone ordering for
appropriate simple orders where the business explicitly enables it. The
founder refinement (authority 1) wins: when enabled, the receptionist gains
read-catalogue / build-cart / create-order tools that call the same Orders
service with actor_type=ai_employee; the MD's WhatsApp-link path remains the
handling for long, custom, negotiated, high-risk or low-confidence orders, and
"no payment details by voice" (MD §11.4) holds unchanged.
